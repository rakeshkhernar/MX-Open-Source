# MX (Manipulation Index) Privacy Policy

MX does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data.

All content analysis runs entirely within your browser using local pattern matching. No text, browsing history, analysis results, or any other data is ever sent to any server, API, or third party.

MX makes zero network requests. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no ads.

The only data MX stores is your personal preferences (indicator position, size, opacity, sensitivity level, and disabled sites list) using your browser's built-in sync storage. This data stays within your browser's own sync infrastructure and is never accessible to the extension developer or any third party.

MX requires only two browser permissions:
- **activeTab**: To read the visible text content of the page you're currently viewing, so it can analyze it for sentiment and manipulation patterns
- **storage**: To remember your personal settings between sessions

Your browsing activity, page content, and analysis results never leave your device.
